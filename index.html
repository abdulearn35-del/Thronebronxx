Frequently Asked

How long does it take to deploy an AI agent? +

Most voice or chat agents go live in 1–3 weeks depending on integrations, call flows, and how much of your existing SOPs and CRM structure are ready to plug in.

Will the AI agent sound robotic on calls? +

No — we use enterprise-grade voice models tuned for natural pacing, interruption handling, and tone, trained on your offer and FAQs so it responds like a real front desk.

Does this replace my team or support them? +

It supports them. The agent handles repetitive front-line work — intake, qualification, follow-up — and hands off to a human the moment a conversation needs real judgment.

Which tools and CRMs do you integrate with? +

Most major CRMs, calendars, and communication platforms — including HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Salesforce, Google Calendar, WhatsApp Business, and custom APIs on request.

What does working together actually look like? +

We start with a free strategy call to map your workflow, propose the right agents and automations, then build, test, and deploy — with reporting so you can see exactly what's running.
